The context

A problem worth solving.

Support representatives answer very different situations: care questions, public reviews, damage claims and card disputes. Each needs the right policy, voice and numbers, and each case can run over several messages.

My contribution

What I brought to the work.

Built the four support apps, the shared chat and case history, and a Chrome sidepanel that brings three of the apps (inquiries, reviews and claims) into the support tools the team already uses. Migrated the platform’s serving layer from AWS Lambda to Firebase and Google Cloud.

The product

What it makes possible.

01

Four task-focused apps

Inquiry, Reviews, Claims and Chargeback each combine a form with a chosen brand voice, audience and knowledge articles, then produce an editable draft.

02

Numbers the reply can trust

In the web app, Claims calculates the refund and the store-credit alternative from the damaged products, and the drafted reply quotes those figures exactly.

03

Knowledge the representative confirms

Articles are matched by keywords and tags and shown before drafting, so the representative sees what the reply will rely on.

04

One case, start to finish

Input, AI draft, edits, regeneration and follow-ups are saved as one case that can be reopened in its original app.

05

Where the team works

A Chrome sidepanel recognizes highlighted text on approved support sites and offers the right form, a quick rewrite in the company voice, or a follow-up drafted from the case history.

Engineering choices

The decisions behind the interface.

01

Calculate first, then write.

The form computes refunds and credits; the AI is asked to quote those figures, not to work them out.

02

Keep a case together.

Every step of a case is stored in order, so a later reply starts from what was already said and changed.

Forty user-visible functions were documented across the apps, chat, history, extension and administration.

Where it stands

In use by the support team since January 2026.

In production since January 2026. The saving comes from retiring the paid AI writing tool the team used before; the figure is my own estimate, not a company or audited number. A number does not cover the rest of the change: replies now start from the team’s own knowledge and voice, refund figures are calculated before a word is written, and every case leaves a history the next person can pick up.
