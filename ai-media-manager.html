The context

A problem worth solving.

Social research starts with a usable collection of accounts and posts. The idea was one place where a brand’s own posts, the posts its customers tag it in and what is working in its field can be looked at side by side, before any AI drafting is layered on top.

My contribution

What I brought to the work.

This is the third build in the same area, each replacing the last. The first was a drafting tool: choose a platform, read an AI strategy summary and post ideas, and get a draft rewritten to sound natural. The second was a larger planning dashboard with creator outreach, a week planner and post ideas with reasons, which reached a clickable design before its foundations were dependable. The rebuild goes in phases, each checked before the next. Sign-in, organisations and roles were checked locally and live. The Instagram phase is built but not yet signed off: accounts added by their public username, a media library of imported posts, and a sync engine that drives a headless browser, saves each slide and uses a vision model to read each post page and label each saved slide.

The product

What the current build does.

The rebuild is the third build in this area. Every screen of all three builds follows, each chapter labelled with what it is.

01

Organisations and roles

One sign-in can belong to several organisations. Each has an owner, admins and members, and an invitation stays pending until it is accepted.

02

Connected accounts

An organisation’s Instagram accounts sit on one page, added by their public username, each with its followers, post count and sync status.

03

The media library

The account’s own posts in one grid, and a second grid set aside for posts it is tagged in. Opening a post steps through a carousel’s slides with its like and comment counts and caption.

04

Sync status

Each account shows when it last synced and how long that took, and a sync that crashes or stalls is flagged.

Build one · March 2026

The Social Command Center: a read-out, ideas and a draft.

A working drafting sandbox · it drafts, a person copies and posts

The first version drafts posts. Choose a platform, read an AI strategy summary and six post ideas, pick one, and get a draft that is written, checked for machine-sounding habits and rewritten, then shown as it would look on that platform. It reads no account numbers and posts nothing. These screens are the tool running on my own machine on 1 October 2026, pointed at an invented brand, so the read-out, the ideas and the draft are its real output for that brand.

Where it stands

A prototype, clearly labeled.

Two parts are unfinished. Importing: capturing every slide of a carousel is not yet dependable, a reel is saved as a still picture and not its video, a sync that finishes with gaps is still marked as synced, and bringing in the posts an account is tagged in is not built, because Instagram shows them only after sign-in. And keeping each organisation’s data private to its own members is not finished. Finding what is trending in an account’s field, and AI drafting on top of the library, come after that.
