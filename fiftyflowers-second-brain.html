The context

A problem worth solving.

Company knowledge was spread across apps, documents and people. Each team needed AI that could use the right part of it and show where an answer came from.

My contribution

What I brought to the work.

Built the Second Brain workspace: team brains around each person’s memory, the Engineering Brain view of the shared repository that AI coding agents work through, and a chat that routes each question to the knowledge it needs.

The product

What it makes possible.

01

Team brains

Each person’s own memory sits at the center with seven team brains around it. Two hold knowledge today: Customer Service, which already powers the production support apps, and Engineering. Marketing is forming and four are still empty.

02

Answers with their sources

A small router model picks the brains a question needs, readers pull the top pages, and the answer lists the pages it consulted.

03

A memory that grows

Add a chat export or a PDF (PDF upload is still to be confirmed on the live site), ask it to research a topic with at most two web searches, or run a check-up for stale and contradictory pages.

04

Engineering Brain

The shared engineering repository as it stood at the end of July 2026, 135 pages and 854 links, appears as a navigable constellation with a reader. The repository has since grown to 261 pages; bringing the view up to date is the next step.

05

Voice and gestures

Speech in and out, a live spoken conversation, and camera-based hand tracking to turn and zoom the knowledge map.
