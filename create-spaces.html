The context

A problem worth solving.

Useful sales data lives inside business software. Before a conversational dashboard can be dependable, exports must land somewhere the analysis workflow can actually read.

My contribution

What I brought to the work.

Tested AI analysis of sales reports exported from Hedberg, with the baseline exercised locally and in the cloud. The work separates reliable data collection from the future conversational interface.

The product

What it makes possible.

01Export the source report+

A bookings-report workflow selects the report options, generates the preview and exports a file to an accessible destination.

02Validate the result+

Compare the exported report and analysis with the known baseline before introducing recurrence.

03Build toward conversation+

A separate AI workspace is intended to let the team ask questions of the shared data and shape useful visualizations.

The work keeps moving.
